      Model input is exactly what the model sees: the scan resampled to 2 mm and cropped to the chosen region. Original (O) shows the full-resolution scan with that crop outlined. If the anatomy is cut off, try the other region. Keys 1–4 switch the display window (the model always reads the full HU range); scroll or use the arrow keys to change slice. Drag the divider to resize, or press F to expand. Hover to read HU; R and E draw a ruler or an oval ROI (Delete removes the last one). On the model input, measurements are approximate (about ±2 mm, HU blurred at edges); use Original for full resolution.
